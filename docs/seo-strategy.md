# Local SEO Strategy

## Objective

The objective of the SEO work was to improve the visibility of the Allforliz website in local search results and help potential customers find the company when searching for painting and remodeling services in Central Florida.

## Target Locations

The SEO strategy focused mainly on:

- Clermont
- Orlando
- Winter Garden
- Lake County
- Orange County
- Surrounding areas in Central Florida

## Main SEO Actions

The work included:

- Creation of dedicated service pages
- Optimization of page titles
- Optimization of meta descriptions
- Use of local keywords
- Creation of location-based content
- Improvement of heading structure
- Internal link organization
- Blog content creation
- Page indexing
- Monitoring through Google Search Console
- Alignment between website content and Google Business Profile

## Service and Location Strategy

The content was structured to connect each service with relevant local search terms.

Examples included:

- Interior painting in Clermont
- Exterior painting in Orlando
- Bathroom remodeling in Winter Garden
- Kitchen remodeling in Central Florida
- Flooring installation in Lake County
- Drywall repair in Clermont and Orlando

The goal was to create useful and natural content without excessive keyword repetition.

## Service Pages

Dedicated pages were created for the main services offered by the company.

These pages were designed to:

- Explain each service clearly
- Improve user navigation
- Target specific search terms
- Support local search visibility
- Connect visitors to estimate request forms

## Blog Strategy

Blog content was created to support the service pages and answer common customer questions.

The articles focused on topics such as:

- Painting preparation
- Interior painting
- Exterior painting
- Kitchen remodeling
- Bathroom remodeling
- Flooring installation
- Home renovation
- Local service information

## Page Indexing

New and updated pages were submitted for indexing through Google Search Console.

The indexing process included:

- Verifying page availability
- Requesting indexing
- Monitoring indexed pages
- Identifying pages with indexing issues
- Reviewing page structure and content when necessary

## Internal Linking

Internal links were added between:

- Service pages
- Blog articles
- Estimate forms
- Main navigation pages
- Related content

This helped improve navigation and created stronger connections between related topics.

## Tools Used

- Google Search Console
- Google Business Profile
- Website content management platform
- SEO analysis tools
- Keyword research tools
- Google search results

## Results

The SEO work contributed to:

- Better organization of the website
- More service-specific pages
- Improved local keyword coverage
- Increased search engine visibility
- Better connection between website pages
- Stronger support for local customer acquisition
